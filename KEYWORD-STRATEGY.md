# Keyword Strategy & Page Intent

Based on the Google Keyword Planner export (5,240 keywords), we have clustered the search terms into the following core intents.

## Keyword Clusters & Monthly Volume (Commercial / Non-Brand)
- **Opener**: ~5,790,400 searches/mo (Excludes ~1.5M branded searches like Chamberlain, Genie, LiftMaster)
- **Repair**: ~4,695,450 searches/mo
- **Replacement**: ~895,900 searches/mo
- **Spring**: ~559,350 searches/mo
- **Installation**: ~512,900 searches/mo
- **Commercial**: ~39,500 searches/mo
- **Emergency**: ~13,350 searches/mo

## Page Strategy
1. **National Service Pages**: We will create high-quality, comprehensive national pages for all the major clusters above (e.g., `/garage-door-repair/`, `/garage-door-opener-repair/`).
2. **Service + Location Pages**: We will combine the high-demand services with `priority` locations.
    - Example: `/texas/garage-door-repair-houston/`
    - Example: `/texas/garage-door-opener-repair-houston/`
3. **Prevention of Thin Pages**: Not every service needs a location page if the demand is too low (e.g., emergency). We will focus on Repair, Installation, and Opener Repair/Installation for local city pages first.
