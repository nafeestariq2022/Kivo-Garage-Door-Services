# Antigravity Instructions for Kivo Garage Door Services

## Project Governance
This project follows a strict set of architectural, business, and SEO guidelines for a national rank-and-rent lead-generation website. 
Always consult `PROJECT-TRUTH.md` before making architectural decisions, adding dependencies, or creating new content structures.

### Key Rules
- **No Fake Data**: NEVER fabricate businesses, offices, addresses, employees, reviews, testimonials, licenses, local branches, LocalBusiness entities, or service providers. The website must accurately represent itself as a lead-generation/service-connection brand.
- **Tech Stack**: Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS. Static-first architecture.
- **SEO & Content**: No thin doorway pages. Do not stuff keywords or create thousands of identical city pages. Location pages must eventually have genuinely useful, locally relevant, and differentiated content.
- **Data-Driven Scalability**: The page architecture must be driven by structured datasets (locations, eligibility, services). Do not hardcode thousands of individual city pages.
- **No Unnecessary Dependencies**: Do not install unused packages. Minimize client-side JavaScript.
- **Phase-by-Phase Workflow**: Execute one phase at a time. Always validate and QA before moving to the next.
- **Internal Data Protection**: Do NOT expose internal buyer information, CPLs, routing rules, buyer names, private account information, or other internal monetization data on the public website.
