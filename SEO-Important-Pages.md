# Essential SEO & Lead-Gen Pages for Kivo Garage Door Services

As a national Rank-and-Rent (lead generation) website, the primary goal is capturing high-intent local search traffic and converting it into phone calls for our buyer network. To rank quickly and establish topical authority, the site architecture must include both **transactional** (money) pages and **trust/authority** (E-E-A-T) pages.

## 1. Localized "Money" Pages (High-Intent / Transactional)
These are the bread-and-butter of a rank-and-rent site. They target users actively searching for immediate help in specific locations.

*   **City-Specific Service Pages (`/{state}/{service}-{city}`)**
    *   *Examples:* `/texas/garage-door-repair-dallas`, `/california/emergency-garage-door-repair-san-diego`
    *   *Why:* Lowest competition, highest conversion rate. People searching these terms need a technician right now. These drive the actual phone calls we sell.
*   **State Hub Pages (`/{state}`)**
    *   *Examples:* `/texas`, `/california`
    *   *Why:* Acts as a geographic silo to pass link equity down to the city pages. Ranks for broader "[Service] in [State]" terms.
*   **National Service Pages (`/services/{service-slug}`)**
    *   *Examples:* `/services/spring-replacement`, `/services/opener-repair`
    *   *Why:* Builds overall topical authority for the specific technical services being offered. Acts as a parent hub for the local variations.

## 2. E-E-A-T & Trust Pages (Crucial for Google Rankings)
Google's algorithm requires sites to prove they are legitimate entities. For a lead-gen site (which cannot use fake addresses/names per our Project Truth guidelines), these pages are vital to avoid being classified as "thin affiliate/doorway" spam.

*   **About Us (`/about`)**
    *   *Why:* Explains the business model transparently (e.g., "Kivo connects homeowners with verified, local garage door professionals"). Builds trust without fabricating a fake local team.
*   **Contact Us (`/contact`)**
    *   *Why:* A core trust signal. Must include a working phone number, email, and ideally a central corporate address or PO Box.
*   **Privacy Policy & Terms of Service (`/privacy`, `/terms`)**
    *   *Why:* Absolutely mandatory for SEO trust, passing Google Core Updates, and running Google Local Services Ads or PPC campaigns if needed.
*   **Disclaimer / How It Works**
    *   *Why:* Protects the site legally and clarifies to the user that we are a connection service, matching them with independent contractors.

## 3. Topical Authority & Supporting Content (Informational)
To rank the "Money" pages, the site needs supporting informational content to prove to Google that Kivo is a subject matter expert in garage doors.

*   **Blog / Resource Center (`/blog` or `/resources`)**
    *   *Why:* Captures top-of-funnel traffic and provides internal links to the local city pages.
    *   *Key Topics to Cover:* "How much does it cost to replace a garage door spring?", "Why is my garage door opening halfway?", "Chain drive vs. Belt drive openers."
*   **FAQ Sections (On Service/City pages)**
    *   *Why:* Targets "People Also Ask" (PAA) snippets on Google and naturally injects long-tail LSI keywords into the local pages.

## 4. B2B Acquisition Pages (For Selling the Leads)
Since the goal is to monetize the traffic, the site also needs to attract contractors willing to buy the calls.

*   **Join Our Network / Partner Program (`/contractors` or `/partner`)**
    *   *Why:* A dedicated landing page for local garage door businesses to sign up to receive leads. This automates the B2B outreach process.
