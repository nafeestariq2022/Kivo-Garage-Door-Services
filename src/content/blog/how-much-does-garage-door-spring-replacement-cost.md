# How Much Does Garage Door Spring Replacement Cost in 2026?

Garage door spring replacement costs $250 to $400 on average for parts and professional labor. Torsion springs cost $75 to $150 each. Extension springs cost $30 to $50 each. Professional labor adds $100 to $200 per service call. Replacing both springs simultaneously prevents future imbalance and additional service fees.

## What Factors Influence Total Replacement Cost?

4 main factors determine the final price of replacement.
- Spring type required for the specific door weight.
- Number of springs needing replacement.
- Labor rates in your specific zip code.
- Emergency or after-hours service fees.

## How Much Do Torsion Springs Cost?

Torsion springs cost $75 to $150 per spring for the parts alone. These springs sit above the door opening and use torque to lift heavy doors. Heavy double doors require 2 torsion springs for balanced operation. Commercial torsion springs cost $150 to $300 each due to larger wire gauges.

## How Much Do Extension Springs Cost?

Extension springs cost $30 to $50 per spring. These springs stretch along the horizontal tracks on both sides of the door. Safety cables cost an additional $15 to $20 and prevent injury if the spring snaps. Extension springs wear out faster than torsion systems.

## What Are Typical Labor Charges?

Professional technicians charge $100 to $200 for 1 to 2 hours of labor. Emergency service calls outside normal business hours add $50 to $100 to the total bill. A standard [garage door spring repair](/services/garage-door-spring-repair) takes 45 to 60 minutes for an experienced technician. Labor rates vary by geographic region and company size.

## Why Should You Replace Both Springs Together?

Replacing both springs at the same time saves $50 to $100 in future labor costs. Springs wear out at the exact same rate. When 1 spring breaks, the second spring will typically fail within 6 months. A balanced door prevents premature wear on the garage door opener motor.

## Can You Replace Garage Door Springs Yourself?

DIY spring replacement saves $100 to $200 in labor but carries significant safety risks. Torsion springs hold extreme tension and cause severe injuries if mishandled. Specialized winding bars cost $15 to $25 and are required for safe tension adjustment. Professional installation includes a warranty on parts and labor.

## How Long Do Garage Door Springs Last?

Standard garage door springs last 10,000 cycles. 1 cycle equals 1 full opening and closing of the door. A household opening the door 4 times daily will get 7 years of life from standard springs. High-cycle springs cost $50 to $100 more but last 20,000 to 50,000 cycles.

## What Are The Signs Of A Broken Spring?

3 clear indicators signal a broken garage door spring.
- A loud bang originating from the garage.
- The door refuses to open using the automatic opener.
- A visible gap appears in the coils of the torsion spring.

## How Can You Extend Spring Lifespan?

Annual maintenance adds 2 to 3 years to spring life. Lubricate springs every 6 months using a lithium-based garage door lubricant. WD-40 attracts dirt and ruins the spring coils. Professional inspections identify wear before a catastrophic failure occurs.

## What Additional Repairs Might Be Needed?

Broken springs often damage related hardware components. Center bearings cost $20 to $35 and wear out alongside torsion springs. Frayed cables cost $15 to $25 and require immediate replacement. Replacing worn rollers costs $40 to $80 and ensures smooth operation after spring replacement.

## How Do Different Climates Affect Spring Durability?

Extreme temperature changes reduce the structural integrity of steel garage door springs. Cold weather causes metal coils to become brittle and snap prematurely. High humidity introduces rust that degrades the wire gauge over time. Applying protective spray prevents oxidation in coastal or damp environments.

## What Are The Warranty Options For New Springs?

Reputable service companies offer 1 to 5 years of warranty coverage on spring replacement. Standard warranties cover parts for 3 years and labor for 1 year. Premium packages include lifetime warranties on the springs but still require labor payments after the first year. Reviewing contract details prevents unexpected out-of-pocket expenses later. Always ask for written warranty documentation. Keep the original invoice in a safe place for future reference.
