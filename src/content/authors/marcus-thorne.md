Marcus Thorne is the Lead Garage Door Technician at Kivo. With over 15 years of hands-on experience in the field, Marcus specializes in high-tension torsion spring systems, smart opener integrations, and structural track realignment. He holds multiple industry safety certifications and has personally overseen the repair and installation of thousands of residential garage doors across the country. 

When he isn't out in the field diagnosing complex mechanical failures, Marcus writes detailed troubleshooting guides to help homeowners understand their garage door systems, prioritize safety, and make informed decisions about repairs and replacements.
