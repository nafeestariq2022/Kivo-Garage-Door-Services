The springs are the most critical mechanical component of your garage door system. They counter-balance the massive weight of the door, allowing it to open and close smoothly. When a spring snaps, the door becomes an immovable hazard that traps your car and compromises your schedule. Because of the extreme tension involved, spring replacement must be handled by trained professionals equipped with the right tools. Professional service safely restores the lifting power of your door and prevents damage to the opener motor.
